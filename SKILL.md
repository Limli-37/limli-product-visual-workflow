# Limli Product Visual Workflow

## Purpose

Help e-commerce brands, fashion businesses, and creative teams plan structured product visual concepts and content workflows with Limli.

## Workflow

1. Identify the product, product category, and target audience.
2. Define the desired visual style and creative direction.
3. Determine whether a model should be used and describe the appropriate model context.
4. Select a suitable environment, background, and setting for the product.
5. Define the required product shots, compositions, and visual variations.
6. Organize the visual concept into clear instructions that can be used to create the desired product visuals.
7. Review the concept to ensure that the product remains the main visual focus and that the direction matches the intended audience and brand style.

## Output

Provide a structured visual concept containing:

- Product and category
- Target audience
- Visual style
- Creative direction
- Model requirements
- Environment and background
- Shot concepts
- Visual variations
- Content direction

## Guidelines

Keep visual concepts relevant to the product, brand style, and intended audience. Prioritize clear, practical, and specific visual directions. Avoid unnecessary details, unrealistic requirements, or generic concepts that do not contribute to the intended product presentation.

When applicable, structure the concept so it can be used as a starting point for creating product visuals with Limli.
